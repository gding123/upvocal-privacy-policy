Privacy Policy

Android app “Upvocal” (com.orion.Upvocal) · Last updated: May 2024

Upvocal is an audio recording, vocal mixing, and voice enhancement app built for singers, rappers, and vocal artists. It allows you to record your voice or import audio files, apply mixing effects, enhance sound quality using artificial intelligence (powered by NVIDIA Studio Voice), and download/export the final master to your device. This policy outlines how your audio data and personal information are handled.

In short: You retain 100% intellectual property and ownership over your voice and music. Microphone access is strictly used when you record. AI processing is secure and ephemeral: your audio files are never used to train public AI models, nor are they sold to third parties. No ads, no data tracking brokers.

1. Data Controller

Upvocal is published independently. For any privacy inquiries, questions, or requests regarding your data, contact: orionextensions@gmail.com.

2. Device Permissions & Local Processing

Upvocal requests explicit permissions to deliver its features:

  • Microphone (Audio Recording): used exclusively when you start a recording session inside the app. Upvocal never records background audio or listens without your direct interaction.
  • Audio Files & Storage: the app uses Android’s secure system file picker to let you import beats and vocal stems (WAV, MP3, etc.) and save/download your final rendered tracks. The app has no broad access to your private files or personal photos.
  • Local Projects & Settings: your custom effect settings, equalizer curves, and local project drafts stay on your phone.

3. AI Voice Enhancement (NVIDIA Studio Voice)

When you choose to enhance an audio track with our AI engine (background noise removal, acoustic echo cancellation, studio-grade clarity):

  • Encrypted Transmission: the selected audio segment is sent securely over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to our processing server running NVIDIA Studio Voice technology.
  • Ephemeral Processing: audio is processed in real time and sent straight back to your device. The temporary audio buffer is deleted immediately after the render is complete.
  • Voice Protection & No AI Training: your voice and stems are never used to train generative AI models, nor are they stored or shared for commercial reuse.

4. User Account (Optional)

Upvocal can be used entirely without an account. If you choose to create one to back up your custom vocal presets:

  • We store your email address and an encrypted password (handled via Supabase authentication).
  • This information is solely used to sync your preferences and vocal presets across your devices.

5. Crash Reports and Diagnostics (Optional)

You can enable or disable anonymous diagnostics in the app settings:

  • Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google): collects error logs and technical specs (phone model, Android version) when a crash occurs. No audio files or recordings are ever included.
  • Usage Metrics (Firebase Analytics, Google): measures overall feature usage anonymously to help prioritize performance updates.

7. Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upvocal claims zero rights over your creations. You remain the sole owner of all copyrights, vocals, lyrics, and audio masters created, imported, or enhanced within the app.

8. Data Retention

  • Local Audio Files: stored on your device until you manually delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Audio Sent for AI Processing: deleted immediately once rendered and delivered to your phone.
  • Account Data (if applicable): stored until you request account deletion.

Under GDPR and international privacy regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, port, and delete your data at any time.

  • To delete your account directly, use the Account Deletion Page or the in-app settings.
