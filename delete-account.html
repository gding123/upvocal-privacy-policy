Delete your Upvocal account

Android app “Upvocal” (com.orion.Upvocal)

This page explains how to delete your Upvocal account and associated data. An account is completely optional: if you never signed up, no personal data is stored on our servers.

1. Directly in the App (Fastest)

  1. Open Upvocal and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Navigate to the Settings / Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

2. What is Deleted vs. Kept

  • Permanently deleted from our servers: your account login (email and hashed password) and any synced vocal mixing presets.
  • Remains strictly on your phone: your imported audio tracks, recorded takes, and exported mixes. These are stored locally and were never uploaded to our servers.

3. Cannot access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app, enter your email below to send an account deletion request:

Clicking the button will open your email client with a pre-filled deletion request.
